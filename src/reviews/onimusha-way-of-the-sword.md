Onimusha: Way of the Sword, released on September 3, 2026, is Capcom's major revival of the classic action-adventure franchise after a 20-year hiatus. The game follows the legendary samurai Miyamoto Musashi—whose face model is based on the iconic late actor Toshiro Mifune—as he battles demonic underworld creatures known as Genma in an Edo-period Kyoto twisted by Malice. Equipped with a mystical Oni Gauntlet, players absorb enemy souls to upgrade abilities while mastering a weighted, highly satisfying combat system based on precise parries, counters, and dodges.

The game debuted to widespread critical acclaim, earning a Metacritic average of 85, and instantly topped the Japanese Famitsu sales charts while surpassing 1 million units sold globally on its launch day. Critics have heavily praised its gorgeous environments, fluid animations, and fluid hack-and-slash mechanics, though some noted minor pacing dips and generic side activities across its 25-to-30-hour runtime.
