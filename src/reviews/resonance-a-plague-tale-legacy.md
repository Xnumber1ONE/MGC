Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy successfully shifts the franchise's formula by trading the rat-infested stealth of the mainline games for an aggressive, fast-paced melee combat system set on the sun-drenched island of Crete [GvoAZ8k_omA]. Following a younger Sophia from A Plague Tale: Requiem, the 12-to-15-hour campaign dives deep into her childhood trauma and a mythical treasure hunt. Backed by an exceptional soundtrack, the narrative brings a refreshing cinematic energy to her backstory, even if the supporting characters lack the emotional weight of Amicia and Hugo.

The gameplay relies heavily on environmental puzzles and Tomb Raider-style platforming, which beautifully complement the breathtaking Mediterranean vistas. However, while the new sword-and-dagger combat feels satisfying initially, it can grow repetitive and bare-bones by the final acts.

Ultimately, it is a bold and worthwhile spin-off that rewards fans with great world-building, currently holding a strong 78–80 on Metacritic.
