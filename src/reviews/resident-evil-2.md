Resident Evil 2 is one of the best remakes ever made. Capcom completely rebuilt the original while keeping the core experience that made it so memorable.

The atmosphere is fantastic, especially inside the Raccoon City Police Department. Every dark hallway feels dangerous, and the limited resources make you think twice before wasting ammunition. The puzzles, exploration, combat, and enemy encounters all work together perfectly to create a constant feeling of tension.

Mr. X is also one of the highlights. Hearing his footsteps getting closer while you're trying to explore or solve a puzzle is genuinely terrifying.

With great visuals, sound design, gameplay, and replayability through its different scenarios, RE2 is an easy recommendation for both horror fans and newcomers.
